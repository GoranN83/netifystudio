Before you get in touch

Commonquestions.

Ask a question
How much does a website cost?

A landing page starts at €590, a business website at €1,490 and a website with a blog in three languages at €2,590. You get the exact price in writing after a free consultation, and it doesn't change afterwards.

How long does it take?

A landing page takes 1–2 weeks, a business website 2–3 weeks and a larger website 3–5 weeks. The timeline also depends on when we receive texts and photos.

Why do you cost more than offers in ads?

For a simple business-card website, a cheaper offer may be enough. Our price includes a conversation about your business, a content plan, design and testing on every screen. Work out what one new client is worth to you, because a website should bring them in for years.

Can I edit the content myself?

Yes, if you want to. In that case we build the website in WordPress and Elementor, and you get a short editing guide. If you leave the updates to us, we build it with modern technologies such as Astro, Next.js and React. Such a website loads faster, and we make all the changes.

What do I need to get started?

Just a short conversation. In the consultation we talk about your business and goals, and afterwards you get a recommendation and a written proposal. We agree on texts and photos later.

What is paid separately?

Domain, hosting and monthly maintenance. The proposal states clearly who arranges and pays for them.
