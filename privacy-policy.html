Privacy policy.

This page explains which details we collect when you send us an enquiry, why we use them and what your rights are.

Who processes the data

Your data is processed by Netify Studio, a web design and development studio. For any question about your data, call us on +381 63 489 978.

What we collect

Through the contact form we collect only what you enter yourself:

  • your full name,
  • your email address,
  • your phone number (optional),
  • the content of your message.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, arrange a consultation and send you a proposal. We do not send you marketing messages and we do not use your details for any other purpose.

Legal basis

We process your details at your request to contact you, that is, to take steps before entering into a contract, in line with the Personal Data Protection Act of the Republic of Serbia.

How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry while we are in contact, and for no longer than 12 months after the last contact. If we work together, we keep the details for as long as the law requires for business records.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or pass on your details to third parties. Only the providers that run our website and email (hosting) can access them, and only as far as that service requires.

Cookies and fonts

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to that service in order to download them.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your details, to restrict their processing, or object to it. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can contact the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection.

Changes

We may update this policy when we change the way we work. The date of the last change is always shown below.
